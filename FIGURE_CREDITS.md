## Editorial policy

Main scientific statements are accompanied by direct citations in each study narrative. Hypothesis-generating numerical results are described as simulations, not validated clinical effects. Preliminary AI outputs and final published figures are distinguished explicitly.

## Layout and editorial update — V9

Research sections now group complementary panels from **the same publication** into a single two-column narrative. This avoids presenting the ERK/p38 dormancy and tumor-immunoediting panels, the platelet-calcium panels, or the viral-ISG panels as independent papers. The introductory section no longer repeats a published study image. Related publications appear separately as brief cross-referenced prose.
